Cloud Reliability Platform

This product focused on creating a unified incident management and service health platform to reduce tool fragmentation and improve response time across cloud service teams. I led the end-to-end design, including discovery, ideation workshops, competitive analysis, design, research, and testing, as well as defining reusable templates to support scalability across the platform.

The work addressed challenges caused by disconnected tools and inconsistent workflows, enabling teams to operate more efficiently through a cohesive and consistent experience.

ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Lead Desginer
  • Led end-to-end design process.
  • Completed 400+ screens and led two official OCI Design Reviews.
  • Conducted competitive analysis and research to inform design decision.
  • Facilitated workshops, user interviews, and usability testing with 20+ participants across business domains.
  • Defined personas and user goals based on research
  • Collaborated closely with product owners, engineers, and scrum masters to align on direction and delivery.
  • Designed reusable templates to scale across cloud services, improving consistency and efficiency.
USER PROBLEM & PITCH

User Problem

I worked closely with Product Management to define this problem statement before we presented the concept to leadership.

"The cloud service teams are operating services with a portfolio of tools and services that support incident/change workflow, communications, visibility and insight. Interacting with individual tools leads to duplication of effort and inconsistency. Innovation by individual teams in this area rarely benefits other teams and organizations that are working to enhance customers' experience."

Pitch visuals
I was actively involved in presenting the pitch to leadership alongside the product and development teams, which led to project approval and funding.

USER RESEARCH

User Interviews and Usability Tests

I conducted comprehensive user research with 5 participants to validate designs and uncover areas for improvement. To structure our research across all 5 participants, I designed two templates that helped standardize data collection across multiple user interviews and usability test sessions.

Usability Testing & Interview Templates

Template 1: Usability Test Task Matrix

Questions, tasks and criteria: Task Group · Task · UI Screen · User Behavior · Product Improvement

This matrix allowed me to track each user's journey through specific tasks, document UI screens where they encountered friction, and directly link observed behaviors to actionable product improvements.

Template 2: User Interview Feedback Card

Questions, tasks and criteria: Name · Role · Overall Rating (1-5) · Area · Scale 1-5 · Notes

Each interview participant received a standardized feedback card where they rated different aspects of the platform on a 1-5 scale and provided qualitative notes. This helped identify patterns across all 5 users and prioritize fixes.

Research synthesis & key iterations: We interviewed 5 users across different roles (SREs, Support Engineers, Delivery Managers). Each session used the Task Matrix to track 8–10 core tasks and the Feedback Card to capture 1–5 scale ratings.

Patterns emerged across all 5 participants: 4 out of 5 struggled with finding customer impact information; average satisfaction for notification workflow was 2.8/5.

Based on these findings, I made the following iterations:

  • Simplified dashboard layout — consolidated 5 views into 3 role-based views
  • Added customer impact preview on incident cards
  • Redesigned notification workflow with template messages and one-click send
  • Added contextual help tooltips for complex workflows
  • Implemented saved filter preferences across sessions
