Design Systems, Templates and Conversion

To address OCI-specific needs, we developed a design system built on top of Oracle's Redwood Design System, along with a set of reusable templates and a new framework. Within two years, the majority of OCI services were transitioned to this standardized approach.

The UX team played a key role in driving adoption by developing templates, supporting teams through the transition, and conducting design reviews. After taking on the role of sole design lead, I drove the completion of the organization-wide transition while guiding the ongoing evolution of the OCI Design System.

ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Design Manager
  • Drove completion of the organization-wide transition to the new framework
  • Led ongoing support and evolution of the OCI Design System, including maintenance, gap identification, and new templates
  • Partnered with Console Product Management to research new patterns, analyze usage data, and iterate on templates
  • Collaborated with engineering to define roadmap, ensure feasibility, and align on delivery timelines
  • Conducted regular reviews with service teams to support migration to the new framework
  • Reviewed design system components and templates with the design team, providing feedback and actionable direction
  • Worked with TPMs to manage JIRA queues, track progress, and ensure delivery across initiatives

OCI Design System

OCI Design Principles

The UX organization defined a set of OCI design principles that were integrated into the OCI Design System to guide consistency and decision-making across products.

Selected Components

OCI required its own design system to address product-specific needs not fully covered by the Redwood Design System. As a result, a number of custom components were created to support these requirements.

Dialog Component Design Guidelines (fragment)

Each component included detailed guidelines that were built directly into the OCI Design System files. These guidelines defined usage, behavior, and interaction patterns, helping ensure consistency and clarity across products.

OCI Templates

Five Templates

In collaboration with engineering and product management, we developed a set of core templates for OCI Console services. These templates were integrated into a unified framework that service teams were required to adopt for new features and updates. This approach improved consistency across the platform while streamlining both design and engineering efforts. Exceptions were allowed only in rare cases, ensuring a more scalable and predictable user experience.

List Template Design Guidelines (Fragment)

We created detailed design guidelines for each template, covering specifications, use cases, dos and don'ts, and responsive behavior.

KEY INSIGHTS

Working on the design system at this scale required defining reusable templates, detailed guidelines, and leading an organization-wide migration. It involved close collaboration across UX, product, and engineering to ensure alignment and consistency, while supporting service teams through reviews, documentation, and edge cases. The successful transition improved both user experience and development efficiency across OCI.
