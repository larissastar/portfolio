National ID

This product enables users to register for and use a Digital ID through a secure, mobile-first experience. Users can onboard by scanning a QR code from a web portal, complete identity verification, and receive a Digital ID stored in a wallet app. Once provisioned, the same wallet experience allows users to securely authenticate into websites using QR codes, biometric verification, and consent-based data sharing.

Digital ID Browser Experience

This experience enables users to register for a Digital ID through a citizen portal using a simple QR-based flow. By scanning the QR code, users are seamlessly guided into a wallet app where they complete identity proofing and onboarding. Once verified, their Digital ID is securely provisioned in the app, creating a streamlined and secure path from registration to activation.

Digital ID Mobile Experience

The mobile wallet experience provides users with secure access to their Digital ID, enabling authentication, biometric verification, and consent-based data sharing. Users can easily manage their digital identity and authenticate into connected websites through QR code scanning directly from the wallet app.

KEY INSIGHTS

This project required close collaboration across teams, as it spanned multiple surfaces including the mobile wallet, citizen portal, and admin console. Regular alignment with product, engineering, security, and front-end teams ensured a cohesive experience. Early partnership with engineering helped validate mobile feasibility and reinforced the importance of consistent patterns across platforms while respecting their constraints.
