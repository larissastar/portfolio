AI Chat

I inherited the AI Chat project after a reorganization, bringing it into my team along with the designer working on it. It was a complex, high-visibility effort that required strong alignment with multiple stakeholders and ongoing iteration.

The work focused on shaping use cases, refining interaction patterns, and grounding design decisions in competitive analysis, while keeping the experience aligned with Oracle's design system and branding.

ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Design Manager
  • Led and supported designers in my team, providing guidance, feedback, and design direction
  • Conducted design reviews to ensure quality and consistency with Oracle's design system
  • Partnered with product managers and stakeholders to define priorities, scope, and user needs
  • Managed intake and oversaw project delivery from initiation through completion
  • Guided design decisions for complex, AI-driven experiences
AI Chat in Use

This section showcases the AI Chat experience integrated into Resource Explorer, highlighting how conversational interactions can support users in navigating and managing cloud resources. Resource Explorer is designed to help users quickly find, filter, and organize resources across Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, improving efficiency when working at scale.

AI Chat Specs

This section outlines the specifications, components, and design system elements used in the AI Chat interface, along with a portion of the engineering handoff documentation. The team worked closely with engineering through multiple touchpoints to ensure alignment, feasibility, and a smooth transition from design to implementation.

KEY INSIGHTS

This work started as a traditional AI chat experience and began evolving into a more integrated, full-page product. The direction shifted toward supporting complex workflows, with a growing focus on AI-driven tasks, recommendations, and deeper integration across Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.
