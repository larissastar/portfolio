Passwordless

Oracle's passwordless sign‑in was a high‑visibility, long‑term initiative focused on improving security and simplifying access. By replacing passwords with secure verification methods like email or mobile authentication, it reduced risk while creating a more seamless user experience. The experience had to work consistently across multiple environments, operating systems, devices, and mobile apps, adding significant complexity.

I was closely involved throughout, participating in extensive collaboration, reviews, and iterations across teams.

You can view a few workflows in this Figma prototype.

ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Design Manager
  • Led cross-functional collaboration through deep involvement in technical discussions with product, engineering, and architects to align vision and ensure feasibility
  • Facilitated ongoing reviews with the Design Director and primary designer to refine work before stakeholder presentations
  • Provided design guidance, feedback, and direction throughout the project
  • Drove alignment across teams through frequent meetings, reviews, and iterative decision-making
  • Helped navigate complex requirements and trade-offs to maintain a cohesive and usable experience
Workflows

In collaboration with product managers, engineers, and architects, we developed a series of workflows to represent key use cases. Each workflow went through multiple rounds of review and iteration to ensure clarity, feasibility, and alignment. A few examples are shown below.

Console Configurations and Settings

Due to its complexity, the project required a dedicated section in the OCI Console for configurations and settings. This area introduced its own set of workflows and patterns to support flexible setup while maintaining a clear and consistent user experience across the platform.

Passwordless screens
Configuring Passkey

The team defined the passkey configuration flow based on technical requirements and industry best practices, ensuring a secure and seamless setup experience for users.

Configuring Authenticator App

This step focused on guiding users through setting up an authenticator app as a secure verification method. The flow was designed to be clear and easy to follow, helping users quickly connect their device and complete setup while meeting security requirements.

Verifying Passkey with Authenticator App

This step verifies the user through a push notification sent to their mobile device. The user simply reviews and approves the request in their authenticator app, completing the verification quickly while maintaining a secure experience.

KEY INSIGHTS

The Passwordless initiative highlighted the importance of strong cross-functional collaboration in a complex, high-visibility project. Ongoing discussions with product, engineering, and architects helped surface constraints early and maintain alignment. Continuous reviews and iteration ensured a balance between security and usability, while the dedicated console configuration area enabled flexible setup. The result was a secure, seamless sign-in experience across environments and devices.
