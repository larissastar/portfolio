Design With AI

This section highlights projects where I used AI to move faster from discovery to execution while keeping the work grounded in UX fundamentals. Since late 2025, I've been integrating AI into my process to accelerate early exploration, synthesize inputs, and iterate on flows, content, and prototypes more efficiently. I also regularly explore new tools through tutorials and hands-on experimentation to stay current and understand when AI meaningfully improves quality and speed.

TOOLS USED ACROSS THE PROJECTS
  • Claude & Claude Design, Figma Make, ChatGPT, Oracle GenAI, OpenAI Codex, Copilot, Google AI, Aura Build, Visily, Subframe, and others.

Oracle Inventory Supply Chain Dashboard
